# SMP M2 HCE evaluator ownership — 2026-09-06

The playing search previously used package-global full-evaluation and pawn caches. Separate `SearchEngine` receivers could therefore read and overwrite each other's evaluator state, while offline Texel model mutation could race searches and leave existing board accumulators, histories, caches, or TT scores tied to an obsolete PST generation.

`SearchEngine` now owns a private HCE evaluator containing the exact existing full-cache and pawn-cache dimensions. `SearchInfo` carries that evaluator explicitly through the hot search and qsearch paths. `SearchSTM` preserves the ordinary cached-white, rule-50 attenuation, side-to-move perspective, and Tempo operation order. `LegacyUndampedSTM` preserves the three alpha-beta safety returns as fresh, undamped HCE evaluations. Direct and Texel evaluation pass a nil pawn cache.

A process-wide HCE lifecycle admits concurrent read leases and rejects mutation immediately with `ErrHCEModelBusy`; it never waits. Search leases span root preparation, recursion, and final callbacks. Successful model publication clears the still-global TT once without resizing it and advances a generation. Each receiver lazily clears its private evaluator caches and complete worker-history family when it next observes a changed generation. Every search admission recomputes the supplied root accumulator, including a different position created before the model swap. Public direct evaluation recomputes a board copy and leaves the caller unchanged.

`ApplyTexelModel` validates, clones, and prebuilds the complete model before reserving mutation, so validation and busy failures change no model, generation, or TT state. Checked coordinate and gradient tuner APIs own the mutation window; legacy signatures panic with `ErrHCEModelBusy` rather than hanging. If a tuner callback panics after a parameter write, the complete captured model and derived PSTs are restored, one invalidation is published, and the original panic resumes.

Focused tests cover score-policy order, all three undamped safety returns, cached versus nil pawn evaluation, concurrent poison isolation between evaluator instances, busy and reentrant entry, failed Apply atomicity, tuner-panic rollback, TT clear timing and size preservation, complete generation invalidation, same-generation warm lifetime, and the old-position model-swap sequence. Full short tests, full short race tests, vet, and build pass. The deterministic cold/warm recorder matches the corrected PVv2 reference exactly for move, score, nodes, PV, histories, terminal positions, special moves, and the legacy node-limit witness; only `identity.requested_source` differs. Evidence is retained in `output/hce-ownership-m2-integration-20260906`.

The TT remains package-global, so full searches on different receivers must not overlap yet. Raw parameter pointers and `RebuildPST` remain offline-only. Raw `ParseFEN` and `Bitboard` make/unmake operations, plus UCI Hash reconfiguration, are outside the HCE lifecycle and must not overlap Apply or tuning. This slice does not wire NNUE, add helper searches, or change search policy.

## Which historical form was ported, and why

The original non-pawn implementation was never committed (it lived in the working tree + a now-gone
`scratchpad/nonpawn.patch`). It was RECOVERED VERBATIM from two dangling git blobs of the reverted
working tree: `f0427c11` (moveorder.go) and `8b0bcc6c` (search.go). That tree carried non-pawn AND a
minor corrector; I ported the **non-pawn corrector ONLY** (the minor term is a separate future T4-lane
item — adding it would be two changes, violating the minimal-variable rule).

Ported = the **ORIGINAL @6245 full-weight form**, not the halve-retry (@3000) and not minor:

- Table: `var nonPawnCorrectionHistory [2][corrHistSize]int` (same size/limit as pawn).
- Key: `nonPawnCorrectionIndex(whiteNonPawn, blackNonPawn)` = hash of each side's non-pawn occupancy
  (`GetWhitePieces()&^pawns`, `GetBlackPieces()&^pawns`; includes the king), distinct murmur3 mixing
  constants so it aliases independently of the pawn table. **Key kept identical to the original.**
- Weight: `* 6245 / 131072` (== the pawn weight; saturated entry ≤ ±49cp).
- Update: byte-identical gravity form to `updatePawnCorrection`; `maybeUpdateNonPawnCorrection` has the
  same in-check / capture-bestmove / directional-signal guards.

Choice reason: (1) the task/revamp-doc Part 3 T4 does not specify NGN-side non-pawn parameters, so the
default is the ORIGINAL @6245 form; (2) the halve to 3000 was a pre-reset over-correction remedy on the
broken base — irrelevant here, and changing the weight would confound the plumbing-fix variable; (3)
minor is a distinct corrector (separate lane).

## Mechanism (diff summary — 3 files, +106/-12)

- **moveorder.go:** add `nonPawnCorrectionHistory` + `nonPawnCorrectionIndex` / `nonPawnCorrectionValue`
  / `updateNonPawnCorrection` / `maybeUpdateNonPawnCorrection` (verbatim from the recovered blob); extend
  `correctedStandPat` to add the non-pawn term (this is the qsearch application — covers both qsearch
  call sites, ~2168 qDepth cap-return and ~2311 stand-pat); add the non-pawn clear loop to
  `ClearHistoryTable`.
- **search.go:** declare `nonPawnCorrIdx`; compute it in the `!inCheck` branch; sum
  `nonPawnCorrectionValue` into `staticEval` at the interior site (~1358) — so `corrStaticEval` (captured
  one line later) becomes raw + pawn + non-pawn, the learn target BOTH tables use; add
  `maybeUpdateNonPawnCorrection(... corrStaticEval ...)` at both update sites (fail-high ~2069 and
  node-complete ~2100).
- **qsearch_test.go:** add `TestNonPawnCorrectionAppliedAtQsearchStandPat` (red→green guard).

Convention alignment vs the pre-reset runs (the entire point): the original blob applied NOTHING at
qsearch and learned from the S7-corrupted `staticEval`. Here the non-pawn term is applied at the qsearch
stand-pat (via `correctedStandPat`) and learns from the pre-S7 `corrStaticEval`, exactly matching the
KEPT pawn term. The `ply>=MaximumDepth` emergency cap stays RAW (untouched).

## Mechanism (one paragraph)

Continuation history before T10b was a 2-ply stack: `continuationHistory` keyed on the move 1 ply back
(opponent's reply context) + `followupHistory` keyed on the move 2 plies back (our own previous move), each
summed 1:1 with the main butterfly `historyTable` in `GetHistoryScore` and in the inlined ordering loop. T10b
adds two more tables — `continuationHistory3` (move 3 plies back) and `continuationHistory4` (move 4 plies
back) — of identical shape `[13][64][13][64]int`, updated with the identical gravity-form step (`gravityUpdate`,
bonus/penalty `depth*depth`) at the same beta-cutoff / quiet-penalty sites, cleared in `ClearHistoryTable`, and
summed with weight 1 (unchanged 1:1:1:1:1 convention — weight changes are the out-of-scope T10c). The 3rd/4th
ancestor moves are read from `info.MoveStack[ply-3]` / `[ply-4]`, guarded by `ply>=3` / `ply>=4` and an
`EmptyMove` check at every read/write site, exactly mirroring the existing `prev2 = MoveStack[ply-2]` plumbing.
No new plumbing beyond the two extra MoveStack reads; probcut's existing not-setting-MoveStack behavior is
inherited unchanged. This is a move-ordering (search-behavior) change: fixed-depth node counts MOVE (nodecheck
below), so it is gated by real-clock games, not proxies.

### Transfer-failure analysis — the +13.1 [S] prior did NOT transfer (measured -2.4 at 8000g)

The Stash #139 prior (+13.1 self-play / +8.8 LTC, the biggest remaining ordering-lane prior) predicted a
solid keep; NGN measured -2.4. This is the first straight prior-transplant *miss* of the T10 lane. Plausible
hypotheses, none yet isolated:

1. **Uniform 1:1:1:1:1 weighting diluted the strong 1/2-ply signals.** T10b deliberately kept the existing
   equal-weight convention (weight tuning was scoped out to T10c). Stash weights its continuation plies
   non-uniformly; summing four tables at flat weight lets the noisier 3rd/4th-ancestor signal wash out the
   sharp 1-ply/2-ply context that already carries most of the ordering value. This is the leading suspect
   because it is exactly the degree of freedom T10b froze.
2. **NGN's existing 2-ply conthist already captures most of the ordering signal.** At NGN's ~84% FMC the
   2-ply stack (`continuationHistory` + `followupHistory`) is already doing the bulk of quiet-move ordering;
   the marginal information in plies 3-4 is small and does not clear the added-noise bar. Stash may have had
   more headroom at the point #139 landed.
3. **+10.56 MiB of new tables cost cache locality at real clock.** Two extra `[13][64][13][64]int` tables
   roughly double the continuation-history footprint; at 10+0.1 bullet the extra L3 pressure can erase a small
   ordering gain that a fixed-depth/fixed-node proxy would not see. The nodecheck moved in mixed directions
   (kiwipete +7.7%, mid -23.7%, end +15.3%) — no consistent EBF win — which is consistent with an ordering
   change that is a wash-to-slightly-negative in tree size and then pays a locality tax on the clock.

### Reopen condition (predeclared)

Reopen T10b **only as a re-derived variant**, never as a straight re-run of the uniform-weight 4-ply form:
either (a) **non-uniform continuation-ply weights** derived via T10c or folded into the T8 SPSA dimension set,
or (b) a **3-ply-only** version (add `continuationHistory3`, drop the 4th table) to test whether the marginal
ply is net-positive before paying for the fourth. A plain uniform-weight re-launch is closed. Note the T3 LMR

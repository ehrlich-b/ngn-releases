## Hypothesis and lineage (T13's reopen path)

T13 (SHELVED 2026-07-09, capped-nonpositive) tried to ADD a qsearch stand-pat fail-high TT store. Its
nodecheck moved +13-39% and the SPRT was flat-negative because the extra depth-0 stores flood the TT and
those depth-0 entries feed the pruning margins. During T13 the mechanism was traced to exactly this S7
site: the refinement at search.go:1368-1374 is **depth-blind**, so any bracketing TT entry — including the
depth-0 capture fail-highs qsearch has written since **a0fdda41 (S3)** — overwrites the main-search
staticEval that RFP / futility / NMP / the improving heuristic consume. T16 is the complementary,
lower-risk lever: instead of changing what qsearch stores, **change what the pruning code TRUSTS** — refine
the static estimate only from genuine search entries (ttDepth >= 1), never from a depth-0 qsearch bound.

**Sign is genuinely unknown — this is a real experiment, not an obvious win.** A qsearch-resolved bound
reflects a settled tactical sequence and may be a *better* static estimate for pruning than a fresh
stand-pat eval; excluding it could tighten margins in a way that helps OR removes a useful sharpening that
hurts. The nodecheck movement (mid -31%, kiwipete +30%, end -17%) shows the pruning tree genuinely
reshapes both directions by position, so the net is an empirical question the SPRT answers.

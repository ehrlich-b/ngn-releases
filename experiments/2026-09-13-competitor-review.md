# NGN against the direct Go competitors

NGN has credible opportunities larger than another sequence of tiny search tweaks. The strongest evidence points to the cost of its Counter evaluation path, the interaction between that evaluator and its inherited search policies, and eventually a stronger existing network. It does **not** identify a single missing algorithm worth the entire deficit. NGN already implements most of the familiar search techniques used by its competitors; Counter 5.5 is stronger despite several simpler choices.

The immediate recommendation is to profile the newly accepted output-kernel engine and admit **one** optimization of accumulator copying or checked transitions if the profile supports it. Do not start a training/data project or another absolute-rating campaign. The classical-era claim that the speed lane was exhausted does not describe today's neural evaluator.

## Evidence boundary

The inspected NGN playing source is `a12e1976981fbf89b148a5b229689bf06916a8ba`, with no engine-source change since the accepted `a51233b` comparison binary. Completed 100-game paired cells put NGN at −323 [−402,−263] against Counter 5.5 at one thread, −413 [−512,−338] against the exact Rodent V1.1 Anand/testers artifact, and −186 [−230,−143] against Counter at eight threads. These are opponent-specific results, not interchangeable widths or an official absolute rating. Roughly 3000–3100 at one thread remains the useful orientation. [Rating reassessment](2026-09-12-rating-reassessment.md).

The September 13 output kernel has since passed its independent audit and been accepted and integrated in `66573f7`: about 2.02× direct-kernel speedup and an equal-weight whole-search time ratio of 0.7215, with all six fixture medians improving. The next-experiment baseline is therefore `66573f7` (`a12e197` plus that exact accepted kernel). This is fresh evidence that a substantial runtime gain was available, not an Elo measurement, a newly established rating or permission to assume another such gain. The [kernel record](2026-09-13-counter-output-kernel.md) owns the exact verdict and provenance.

No new matches, engine runs or benchmarks were performed for this review. Previous profiles and losses are used with their dates and limitations; proposed gains remain hypotheses. All future engine work belongs on WSL. Own-network training, new training data and training infrastructure are deferred.

## What the comparison actually controls

Counter 5.5 is the most informative opponent because NGN uses the same network. Its exact release source is pinned at [`63c487ca`](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/tree/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872). More specifically, NGN reproduces Counter's truncation/clamp, non-pawn-material scaling, rule-50 attenuation and side-to-move conversion. This was checked against [Counter's `EvaluateQuick`, lines 51–61](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/eval/nnue/evaluation.go#L51) and [NGN's adapter, lines 469–489](../engine/worker_evaluator.go#L469). There is no newly discovered missing sign flip or material factor.

Nevertheless, “same network” is not “identical evaluation throughout search.” NGN adds three correction-history terms and may replace its pruning static with a compatible TT bound; Counter's search uses its evaluator directly. Incremental float operation order, release assembly selection, emergency evaluation and time allocation also require exact controls. The gap demonstrates that owning a new network is not a prerequisite to useful progress; it does not prove that all 323 Elo belongs to search. [NGN correction application](../engine/moveorder.go#L53), [NGN TT refinement](../engine/search.go#L1527), [Counter search](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/search.go#L97).

Rodent needs a different distinction. The tested V1.1 executable contains `rodent_anand_512hl.bin`, SHA-256 `5f7480b56538e9e64ee02fb5b4842bf0394ee44b707dae3a63ec7f902abd0afb`, using pure NNUE, scale 192 and horizontal mirroring. It is not the default V1.1 network. Its release-specific source changes were not supplied as a reproducible tag diff, so tagged source is a mechanism reference, not proof of the tested executable's every option. [Exact artifact audit](2026-09-06-rodent-v1.1-release-confirmation.md).

Rodent's V1.1 tag is [`5689d0ba`](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/tree/5689d0babebe95d87592eaaaee73ea555ef9345c). Current source inspected here is [`7b002ae1`](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/tree/7b002ae1e8e8e0d49ac9a05e5ad14c4bdf7b4b30), with four king-input buckets, 768 hidden units and eight output buckets. Those richer current-network features cannot explain the measured V1.1 deficit. V1.2's invalid promotion-PV cell remains failed and contributes no accepted strength evidence. [Current network constants](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/blob/7b002ae1e8e8e0d49ac9a05e5ad14c4bdf7b4b30/nnue.go#L36).

Chess-3 [`76dc30a3`](https://github.com/paulsonkoly/chess-3/tree/76dc30a3f7cf03f64cb74ae7a189a9fa572890ad) and Zahak [`72ff8066`](https://github.com/amanjpro/zahak/tree/72ff8066e2d8348d8d3847fab62c4a6b8344240d) provide direct Go implementation comparisons. There is no matched current NGN result against either in the accepted cells above; this review does not invent a relative rating for them. Stockfish 18 [`cb3d4ee9`](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/tree/cb3d4ee9b47d0c5aae855b12379378ea1439675c) is used only to assess existing-network integration.

### 1. Counter transition and accumulator work

Every ordinary Counter search transition currently decodes and checks the move in `counterMoveDelta`, reconstructs the actual twelve-plane post-board, validates that board, validates the context's current board, derives another post-board, and compares the complete results. It then copies 512 float32 lanes before applying each update row through a separate AVX2 pass. A normal quiet move therefore has a 2 KiB copy and two row passes; the accepted feature kernel loads and stores the accumulator on each pass. These are specific operations to profile, not a claim that all validation should be removed. [Adapter](../engine/counter_transition.go#L27), [post-board reconstruction](../engine/counter_transition.go#L147), [checked push](../countereval/search_context.go#L88), [transition derivation](../countereval/context.go#L208), [row kernels](../countereval/feature_updates_amd64_v3.s#L5).

The smallest promising arithmetic change is a fused **copy plus ordered row updates** kernel: load one block of parent lanes, apply the existing add/sub operations in the same per-lane order, and store the child once. Interleaving independent lanes need not change float32 arithmetic; reassociating updates or using FMA would. Current Rodent has separate source/destination fused move/capture functions, a useful implementation reference, although its integer arithmetic and refresh rules differ. Counter 5.5 itself also copies before its updates, so this is an operation-count hypothesis, not something proven necessary by the Counter matchup. [Current Rodent move update](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/blob/7b002ae1e8e8e0d49ac9a05e5ad14c4bdf7b4b30/nnue.go#L420), [Counter update assembly](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/eval/nnue/nnue_instructions_avx.s#L85).

If checked-transition code instead dominates the remaining cost, optimize repeated work while retaining an explicit invariant: the engine's actual post-move state must still be checked, special moves must remain distinguishable, and rejected transitions must preserve board/context state. The already accepted compact transition adapter delivered a measured cold-search reduction of about 5.47%; this lane has paid once, but that does not establish the size of the next improvement. [Accepted numerical/performance record](2026-09-06-current-status.md#numerical-and-performance-evidence).

The cheapest discriminator is one representative CPU and allocation profile after the output change, covering persistent-engine searches as well as the cold fixture. Quantify copy/update, transition validation, search/move ordering and allocation shares. Pick one mechanism. For a fused kernel, retain exact all-lane, special-move, null/pop, rounding and fixed-search identity checks; require at least 3% repeatable whole-search improvement with no fixture median regression. Shelve on failed identity, insufficient attributable cost or failure to clear the frozen speed gate.

Two nearby facts deserve observation, not simultaneous patches. The search allocates a 256-frame scratch pool anew for each worker/search even though evaluator contexts and histories persist; and board make/unmake still maintains HCE material/PST accumulators under Counter. The former could matter at short clocks, the latter could add per-move work, but neither is presently a demonstrated bottleneck. Profile before proposing reuse or evaluator-specific board updates. [Frame allocation](../engine/search.go#L613), [frame layout](../engine/search.go#L459), [PST maintenance](../engine/bitboard.go#L129).

### 2. NNUE/search-policy interaction

NGN's Counter adapter preserves the pretrained evaluator, but the surrounding pruning/reduction/correction policies mostly descend from the handcrafted-evaluation campaign. The accepted T17+T8 vector was useful on that base; importing Counter did not establish every margin or correction family as optimal for the new score distribution. Counter's corresponding search has different futility eligibility, history scaling and reduction adjustments, and no equivalent three-term correction addition at its static-eval site. This creates a plausible medium-to-large **interaction** opportunity, not evidence that one standard parameter should be copied. [NGN static/pruning input](../engine/search.go#L1504), [Counter pruning and LMR](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/search.go#L216), [accepted T17+T8 evidence](2026-09-05-t8-qcap-confirmation.md).

There is also a concrete depth-policy discrepancy: NGN computes extended `nextDepth`, but later non-first-move scouts begin at `depth - 1 - reduction`. An unreduced non-PV scout can therefore omit the extension entirely. Counter's scout depth is derived from its extended `newDepth`, with its own explicit reduction policy. This is the already known extension/scout joint, not a new discovery or a proved correctness defect. The singular-only interaction already lost its completed gate, so replaying it is a poor next choice. [NGN scout/research logic](../engine/search.go#L2022), [Counter depth flow](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/search.go#L254), [prior singular gate](2026-09-05-singular-t8-confirmation.md).

The cheapest useful diagnostic is to take a deterministic, bounded sample from the **accepted Counter-backed games**, preserve history, and record raw adapted static, correction contribution, TT refinement, relevant prune/reduction counts and completed-iteration decisions. The first candidate should test one supported family—for example the three-term correction family as one declared interaction—rather than stacking a copied futility margin, extension repair and LMR vector. Reference analysis can reject a mechanism hypothesis; it cannot certify Elo or justify keeping a positive but inconclusive result.

Stop if the proposed family seldom affects the sampled decisions, if a fresh diagnostic fails to distinguish the claimed mechanism, or if the predeclared paired-clock gate rejects or caps inconclusively. Do not launch another broad SPSA run merely because the source looks different. The old loss study found 19 unchanged choices and no demonstrated 50-cp rescues at four times the node budget, but used a classical engine and a selected, partially unscorable sample. It neither proves an evaluation cause nor closes runtime/search work today. [Loss-study limitations](2026-09-04-loss-study.md).

### 3. A stronger existing network

The best bounded next import to assess is Rodent V1.1's exact Anand network: its architecture and bytes are already known from the tested opponent. It offers two-perspective integer accumulators, horizontal mirroring and squared clipped activation. It cannot be loaded as NGN-v1 or Counter weights merely because all three involve 768 board features. A separate adapter needs feature orientation, king-move mirror refresh, accumulator arithmetic, activation, serialization, output scale and rule-50 behavior verified against its exact oracle. V1.1's 512×2 int16 accumulator is 2 KiB, the same raw accumulator byte count as Counter's 512 float32 lanes; the arithmetic and feature work differ. [V1.1 network layout](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/blob/5689d0babebe95d87592eaaaee73ea555ef9345c/nnue.go#L38), [feature indexing](https://github.com/nescitus/Rodent-V/blob/5689d0babebe95d87592eaaaee73ea555ef9345c/nnue.go#L190).

Rodent's richer current network is a later option, not an interchangeable V1.1 file. The existing Stockfish 18 work is also substantial but incomplete: SMALL has reference/incremental inference; BIG has loading only. Stockfish normally selects BIG except in large material imbalances, can fall back from SMALL to BIG, and adds complexity/material/optimism/rule-50 adaptation. SMALL alone is therefore not a credible shortcut to the full Stockfish evaluator. [NGN BIG boundary](../nnue/sf18big/doc.go), [Stockfish 18 selection and adaptation](https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish/blob/cb3d4ee9b47d0c5aae855b12379378ea1439675c/src/evaluate.cpp#L49).

The first discriminator is a short written integration contract and cost estimate for one exact existing network. Once chosen, require full-refresh and incremental parity before a realistic throughput check, and playing strength before adoption. Stop the first slice if model provenance, exact scale/feature semantics or a working oracle cannot be established. Do not build a general arbitrary-`.nnue` framework, train replacements, or claim the opponent's rating transfers with its weights. This opportunity is credible but slower to confirm than the current runtime work.

### 4. Useful SMP work

NGN already has real, accepted multicore benefit. At one worker the TT uses direct access, so its multiple-worker mutex path cannot explain the one-thread deficit. At multiple workers, every store acquires a global age mutex and then a stripe mutex; probes take the stripe mutex. The search advances age once before launching workers. An immutable search-generation value might avoid the per-store age lock while preserving coherent entry ownership, but only contention evidence makes it a priority. Counter uses per-entry CAS and skips busy entries; wholesale adoption would change table semantics and needs its own validation. [NGN mode selection](../engine/search_control.go#L137), [TT store/probe](../engine/cache.go#L159), [search generation](../engine/search_smp.go#L94), [Counter TT](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/transtable.go#L79).

Separately, NGN's helpers begin their own iterative-deepening loops and the primary owns the final result. Counter assigns tasks beyond the deepest completed iteration and can adopt a deeper completed result. This makes explicit diversification worth testing after one-thread runtime improvements. Aggregate NPS alone cannot show useful tree coverage; the already accepted 6.41–6.69× HCE throughput scaling is evidence against assuming SMP is fundamentally broken. [NGN helper loop](../engine/search_smp.go#L180), [pinned scheduling comparison](2026-09-06-counter-smp-source-comparison.md), [Counter scheduler](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/lazysmp.go#L58).

First collect bounded one/eight-worker CPU, mutex/block and allocation profiles on the same accepted evaluator. Stop the contention lane if waiting is negligible. If experimenting with depth staggering, preserve one-thread behavior and primary publication, predeclare a small schedule, and require paired-clock strength; test helper-result selection separately. Do not copy Rodent's independently atomic XOR words as a proof of transactional coherence: that is a different correctness contract from NGN's synchronized entries.

## Candidates that should not jump the queue

The large history footprint is real: two `[13][64][13][64]int` tables occupy 10.5625 MiB together on amd64, or 84.5 MiB across eight workers before other state. Their values are bounded to ±8192, and Counter stores histories in `int16`. However, NGN already tested this fourfold shrink and found roughly flat speed on the prior machine/workload. The old record is descriptive, pre-reset evidence, not a permanent prohibition; reopen only on current memory/cache pressure, a changed machine, or a deliberately different eight-worker workload. It is not a fresh discovered win. [NGN history bounds](../engine/moveorder.go#L203), [Counter history arithmetic](https://github.com/ChizhovVadim/CounterGo/blob/63c487ca724c620f71c129d62129c6fb9109c872/pkg/engine/history.go#L55), [speed-log result](speed-log.md).

A genuine lazy-generation picker could save more work than NGN's present selection scheme, as Chess-3 and Rodent demonstrate. But the old deferred-quiet-scoring prototype was slower and changed ordering because history was read after the TT subtree had mutated it; a later exact block picker was about 4% slower. Those failures rule out advertising “make it staged” as free speed. A new design must identify which generation/scoring cost is avoidable and preserve intended tie/history timing, or enter the behavior-change game gate. [Old mechanism and negative result](speed-log.md), [later exact-picker result](2026-09-05-classical-review-update.md).

Time management is already adaptive: NGN has soft/hard budgets, stability and root-node-effort inputs. Its UCI path computes allocation before updating current game-phase state, a known explicitly preserved policy order. That is a legitimate future experiment, not a newly missing time manager. A prior “spend more by dropping projection” experiment lost −7.8 Elo at its completed gate; unused bank alone is not proof of underthinking. Use saved clock traces to show recurrence before opening this lane. [Time policy](../engine/time.go#L183), [UCI order](../engine/uci.go#L889), [completed rejection](2026-07-02-t1a-softstop.md).

Quiet promotions remain absent from the accepted qsearch, but the freshly completed 400-game addition was +12 [−11,+35] and shelved. More continuation history, broader SEE/ProbCut rules, singular changes and clustered TT all need their prior records checked before being proposed again. Functional tablebases remain a real production gap, but no evidence here makes them the explanation for a broad same-network middlegame deficit. [Quiet-promotion verdict](2026-09-12-qpromo-gate.md).

## Specific next experiment

Freeze the immediate source including the accepted output kernel and the existing Counter network. Spend at most one hour profiling its current CPU and allocation costs across the established six fixtures and a small deterministic sample of accepted external-game prefixes, including persistent-game state. This is a prioritization measurement, not recalibration.

If accumulator copy plus feature updates remain a sufficiently large share to plausibly clear a 3% whole-search gain, test only a fused copy/ordered-update kernel. Preserve the checked transition API, feature order and every search policy. Use the already proven raw-bit/oracle/fixed-search identity approach and a predeclared repeated wall-time gate. If that cost is too small, do not implement it: select the measured checked-transition bottleneck instead and write the smallest invariant-preserving change. Do not combine both in the first candidate.

The likely route toward the leaders is several substantial, measured improvements in the evaluator path and search policy, followed by narrower tuning. The source does not support promising hundreds of Elo from one architectural transplant. It does support postponing countless micro-optimizations and all own-network training while these larger, concrete opportunities are tested.

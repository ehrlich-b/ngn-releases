## The (a) call, stated honestly — including the reading that argues the other way

**This deserves to be explicit rather than buried, because the pre-registration is genuinely ambiguous here:
it was written assuming the run would reach the 8000g cap, and it did not.**

**The three numeric sub-thresholds were each individually MET:** |penta| 4.3 <= 5.0, the CI contains 0, and
half-width 6.0 <= 8.0. A reader who takes only that sentence would score (a) as a PASS. That reading is on
the record and is not being hidden.

**It is nevertheless scored FAIL, for three reasons:**

1. **Criterion (a) is written "at the 8000g cap".** That precondition was not met — the SPRT stopped early by
   crossing a bound, which is a categorically different termination than running to the cap with a CI that
   straddles 0.
2. **The trailing clause — "A decisive H1 in either direction is still an outright FAIL" — fires.** The
   phrase "in either direction" only parses if the author meant *any decisive bound crossing*: H1 has one
   direction by definition, so "H1 in either direction" is otherwise meaningless. The run crossed decisively.
3. **The negative offset REPLICATED across two independent A/A runs at this TC**: -8.3 [-20,+4] at 1600g
   (2026-07-03) and -4.3 [-10,+2] at 6922g (today). Weighted across both (~8500 games) the estimate is
   ~**-5.0**, i.e. at or past the 5.0 tolerance. **The 10+0.1 c8 A/A control is -1.1** (2026-07-03 M5
   adjudication gate), so the offset is ~4-5x larger at the halved clock.

**Note the incentive direction, since it matters for trusting this call: a PASS was the CONVENIENT outcome**
(2x on SPSA and cheap Stage-1 filtering). FAIL is the expensive reading, which is why the ambiguity is being
resolved conservatively rather than in the direction that would have accelerated the campaign.

**Mechanism this is consistent with:** an A/A is 0 by construction — same binary, paired openings, colours
swapped — so a persistent offset means the TC interacts with process scheduling. At 5+0.05 with 16 engine
processes on 8 physical cores, scheduling jitter is a much larger fraction of a 5s base budget than of a 10s
one. That is precisely the failure mode M6 existed to detect, and it is now measured twice.

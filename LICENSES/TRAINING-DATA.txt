Historically, NGN 0.3.0's embedded network (SHA-256 c6c127966d1cd9848b8bbe836ae74d5672874a3e74e973a488e4d92e350416f2) was recorded as randomly initialized and trained by NGN's trainer (trainer/) on positions, search scores and game results from that file. No borrowed trained network or optimizer was imported.

Alteration (2026-09-24, one pass): kept quiet, not-in-check positions from train-split chains; excluded positions whose key is in conflicts.bin; dropped the VALUE_NONE score sentinel; remapped scores with a monotone 64-knot piecewise-linear map (score-map.json) fitted against Stockfish 18 evaluations of about 100,000 calibration positions; stored as 2,374,290,033 32-byte records (SHA-256 270f65bdf9f5c948160cae5330030a60f99e94383de2a290b565ae7aad7cde26). The pass was run by an earlier NGN research tool that decoded the binpack with the sfbinpack 0.6.4 crate and identified positions for the conflicts.bin exclusion by Rodent V1.2's network-input layout (Naman Thanki and Pawel Koziol). That tool is not part of NGN 0.3.0, and nothing from it is in the engine, the trainer or the network. The historical 0.3.0 release referred to a separate training-method archive. That archive is not supplied in 0.4.0. See docs/TRAINING-METHOD.md for the retained method, input identities and missing-input limits.

The altered database retains ODbL terms. The engine and network license (GPL-3.0-only) is stated in LICENSE-GRANT.txt and does not replace them.

NGN 0.4.0 embeds NGNN3 H768 (SHA-256 2dcb6d936c0275927cb9e881f42e22558e618ad7e6fd998df6c4e5046d42c443). Its retained manifest names a widened NGN checkpoint and T80, T76 and T60/T70 corpora. Verified lineage starts with fresh NGN trainer revision 7ef9821, H512 export 81215229 and checkpoint eb774fa7; seeded widening preserves the old units and zeroes new output weights. The trained H768 export equals the embedded network. Corpus content identities are pinned in docs/TRAINING-METHOD.md; historical corpus build inputs are not distributed.

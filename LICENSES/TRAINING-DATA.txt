The NGN-owned net was randomly initialized and trained on mapped T80 search scores and game results. No borrowed trained network or optimizer was imported.
The separately supplied ngn-0.2.0-rc.1-training-method.tar.gz contains the complete alteration recipe, exact filter keys and score map, plus source provenance and notices. The corresponding engine source contains the transformation algorithms.
The altered database retains ODbL terms. The engine and model licenses are a separate release decision.
